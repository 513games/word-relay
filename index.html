Translator

Consonant beginnings move to the end and receive -en. Vowel words stay intact and receive an ending based on their first vowel: -ak, -ek, -ik, -ok, -uk. Y acts as a vowel only when a word contains no A, E, I, O, or U.

Examples
always → alwaysak
ear → earek
I → Iik
inside → insideik
open → openok
up → upuk

crap → apcren
car → arcen
quick → ickquen
store → oresten

thy → ythen
fly → yflen
cry → ycren
my → ymen
