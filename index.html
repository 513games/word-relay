How to Play

1
Start with the locked word.
It never moves.
2
Build one continuous chain.
Every neighboring pair forms a familiar phrase or compound word. Each word plays off the next.
FOOT → BALL → PARK → BENCH
3
Tap and move.
Select a word, then move it left or right to fit where you think it goes.
4
Check your work.
Tap Check Progress to see how close you are. Green words are in their correct place. Red words are misplaced.

All Relays are untimed. Take your time, experiment, and see how few Checks you can solve them in.
