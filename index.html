Translator

Vowel-starting words stay in place and receive -en. Consonant beginnings move to the end and receive -en.

Examples

eat → eaten
act → ăcten
elephant → elephănten
open → øpenen
I → Ìen

crap → ăpcren
car → ărcen
store → øresten
quick → ìckquen

thy → ythen
fly → yflen
cry → ycren
my → ymen
