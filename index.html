S-EG Translator

Words beginning with a vowel receive an S at the beginning and EG at the end. Words beginning with consonants move their opening consonant cluster to the end and add EG.

Rules

Vowel-starting words

Add S to the beginning and EG to the end.

I → Sieg am → sameg office → sofficeeg

Consonant-starting words

Move every consonant before the first vowel to the end, then add EG.

happy → appyheg with → ithweg street → eetstreg

Contractions

Keep the apostrophe in place while moving the opening consonant cluster.

can't → an'tceg let's → et'sleg
