Translator

Nouns stay in their original order and receive -en. Other vowel-starting words receive s- and -ek. Other consonant-starting words move their opening consonant cluster to the end and receive -en.

Examples

dog → døgen
person → persønen
house → høuseen
car → căren
friend → ìenden

I → Sìek
am → sămek
open → søpenek
around → sărøundek

think → ìnkthen

probably → øbăblypren
