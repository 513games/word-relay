Translator

Vowel words receive s- and -ek. Consonant beginnings move to the end and receive -en. Y acts as a vowel only when a word contains no A, E, I, O, or U.

Examples

I → Sìek
am → sămek
apple → săppleek
open → søpenek
inside → sìnsìdeek
around → sărøundek

crap → ăpcren
car → ărcen
quick → ìckquen
store → øresten
going → øìnggen

thy → ythen
fly → yflen
cry → ycren
my → ymen
