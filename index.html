S-EG Translator

Words beginning with a vowel receive an S at the beginning and EG at the end. Words beginning with consonants move their opening consonant cluster to the end and add EG. The pair QU is treated as one consonant unit at the beginning of a word. Y acts as a vowel only when a word contains no A, E, I, O, or U. In the translated output, A, E, I, and O are always written as Å, Ę, Ï, and Ø.

Rules

Vowel-starting words

Add S to the beginning and EG to the end. Then convert A, E, I, and O to their marked forms.

I → Sïęg am → såmęg office → søffïcęęg

Consonant-starting words

Move every consonant before the first vowel to the end, then add EG. QU stays together as one consonant pair. Y becomes a vowel only when no A, E, I, O, or U exists.

happy → åppyhęg with → ïthwęg street → ęętstręg quick → ïckquęg thy → ythęg yes → ęsyęg

Contractions

Keep the apostrophe inside the word while moving the opening consonant cluster.

can't → ån'tcęg let's → ęt'slęg I'm → Sï'męg
