Translator

Vowel-starting words stay in place and receive -ay. Consonant beginnings move to the end and receive -ay.

Examples

eat → eăta y
act → ăctay
elephant → elephăntay
open → øpenay
I → Ìay
about → ăbøutay
inside → ìnsìdeay

crap → ăpcray

store → ørestay
